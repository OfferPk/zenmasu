## Full Description (max 4000 characters)
Discover the meditative elegance of Japanese pencil-and-paper puzzles with ZenMasu, a calm, minimalist Masyu (白珠黒珠 - Pearls) loop puzzle designed for focus and tranquility.

In ZenMasu, your goal is to draw a single continuous, non-intersecting loop that connects pearls according to the two timeless Masyu rules:

### ⚪ The White Lotus Pearl Rule
The loop must pass straight through every white pearl without turning. Furthermore, the loop must make a sharp 90-degree turn in at least one of the immediately adjacent cells (before, after, or both).

### ⚫ The Black Obsidian Pearl Rule
The loop must make a 90-degree turn inside every black pearl. Furthermore, the loop must continue straight for at least one full cell in both directions from the black pearl before making another turn.

### ✨ Zen Features
• **50 Handcrafted Levels**: 5 difficulty chapters ranging from gentle 5×5 introductory gardens to complex 9×9 sanctuaries:
  - Spring Garden (5×5)
  - Koi River (6×6)
  - Bamboo Forest (7×7)
  - Stone Path (8×8)
  - Zen Sanctuary (9×9)
• **Procedural Garden & Silk Acoustics**: Pure Web Audio synthesizer with crisp bamboo ticks on segment drawing, gentle water droplet chimes when a pearl constraint is satisfied, silk string koto plucks on segment connection, and a resonant zen temple victory gong upon loop closure. Zero audio sample downloads required.
• **Smart Deductive Hints**: Learn the subtle logic of Masyu with immediate hints for corner pearls, adjacent pearls, and boundary constraints.
• **Fluid Touch Controls**: Drag along grid paths or tap to toggle line segments. Use X-marks to eliminate impossible paths. Unlimited undo and reset.
• **100% Offline & Private**: Zero data collection, no internet connection required, no timer pressure, and no advertisements. Play peacefully anytime, anywhere.

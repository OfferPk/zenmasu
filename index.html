How to play

Draw one continuous, non-intersecting loop through cell centres. Let each pearl guide the path:

◯ White pearl

Pass straight through the pearl. Turn in the cell immediately before it, after it, or both.

● Black pearl

Turn 90° inside the pearl, then travel straight for at least one cell on both sides.

⌁ The loop

Visit every pearl and close a single loop. Empty cells may be straight, turning, or left untouched.

Tip Tap or drag along a grid edge to draw. Switch to Cross to mark an impossible path; right-click also places a cross.

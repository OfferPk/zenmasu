How to Play ZenMasu

Draw a single continuous, non-intersecting loop that connects cell centers orthogonally (horizontally & vertically) and satisfies all pearl constraints:

⚪ White Pearl (Lotus)

• The loop must travel straight through the pearl (no turns inside).
• The loop must turn 90° in the cell immediately before and/or after the white pearl.

⚫ Black Pearl (Obsidian)

• The loop must make a 90° turn inside the pearl.
• The loop must continue straight for at least one cell in both directions from the turn.

🌀 The Noble Loop

• All pearls must be visited by the loop.
• Visited cells form exactly one single closed loop without branches or dead ends.
• Cells without pearls can contain straight paths, turns, or be empty.

Tip: Tap or drag along edges to place lines. Use the Cross tool (or right-click) to mark paths where the loop cannot travel.
